Learning Objectives

Upon completion of this course, students will be able to:

  1. Diagram the reproductive systems, embryonic development, and life histories of insects
  2. Explain the ecological importance of insects and their relationships to plants, animals, and humans
  3. Apply the principles of phylogenetics and systematics to entomological diversity
  4. Construct and interpret evolutionary trees using morphological and molecular data
  5. Classify the major groups of insects from images and specimens
  6. Describe insect anatomy and physiology
  7. Create a quality insect collection of correctly curated and identified specimens
